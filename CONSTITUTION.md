# Constitution — Llama OS Public Skills

## What this is

**Llama OS — Public Skills** is the open, shareable layer of Llama Ventures'
AI-native operating system. It teaches any capable AI agent a piece of how the firm
thinks about venture work — diligence, markets, technology, judgment.

We release it publicly because a written framework is a **brand asset, not a moat**.

## A deliberate subset

This repository is, by design, only a slice. Internally, Llama Ventures runs a fuller
system: a larger library of specialized skills, and **Llama Command** — the backend
where the firm's AI agents work alongside the team on live pipeline, diligence, and
accumulated knowledge.

Those private layers stay private. What lives here is the **methodology** — the part
that is both safe to share and useful on its own.

## Principles

1. **Methodology is a brand asset, not a moat** — so we open it.
2. **AI augments judgment; it does not replace it.** The decision, and the
   responsibility, stay with the human.
3. **Facts and opinions are different things** — always label which is which.
   Grounded subjectivity beats bland consensus.
4. **Built for agents** — every skill is self-contained, tool-agnostic, and reusable
   by any capable AI, with no dependency on a private backend.
5. **A living system** — knowledge compounds; skills are meant to be used, adapted,
   and improved.

## Boundaries

What may and may not live here is governed by [`DISCLOSURE.md`](DISCLOSURE.md): no
information from private sources, no models of specific real people without consent,
nothing about a specific LP / portfolio company / active deal, no personal contact
information, and no internal infrastructure. Examples are illustrative, never real
transactions.

## Evolution

The public layer evolves through contribution — see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
The private system evolves separately. This public layer is curated and reviewed, and
only ever contains what has been cleared for the open.
