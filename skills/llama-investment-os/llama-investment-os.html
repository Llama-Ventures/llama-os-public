第二部分 · 思想

根本命题 · 人机协作的边界

人做可计算的事情应交给 AI；人应该做不可计算的事——其中最核心的是选择。

AI 负责人负责
标准答案 · 事实搜集 · 数据降噪定义问题 · 投资审美 · 最终选择
逻辑一致性检查 · 历史回测识别无法被数据量化的生命力与潜能
选择是人的第一权利，责任是第二。AI 不替代人做决定，AI 让人的判断更具穿透力。

核心信念 · 投资 = 认知的变现

  • 没有超越平均的认知 → 没有超额回报
  • 平庸共识 → 平均回报
  • 认知变现 = "非共识下的正确"
"若非独特，认知便无价值；若非主观，判断便无力量。"

三大原则 · 捍卫"有依据的主观"

  1. 拒绝平庸的客观 —— 不追求人云亦云的统计学正确；挖掘只有你观察到的细节
  2. 建设个体的判断实验室 —— 组织不统一认知，为每个人提供资源和 AI 工具；认知共振而非认知统一
  3. 组织能力是个人认知的总和与涌现 —— 个体独特性是火种，组织系统性是火焰

投资者三项权利

权利要义
错的权利投资能力在真实痛感的错误上建立。犯错是常态，不是意外
无知的权利有勇气承认在绝大多数领域是无知的，将注意力聚焦极少数领域。没有独特认知，就无法成就投资事业
延迟反复的权利允许在"投与不投"之间反复震荡。这不是软弱，是大脑处理高维信息时的调优过程

#纪律要义

第三部分 · 方法

DD 双轨框架 · 事 / 人

看人（六步）

#步骤核心追问
①人物前传不是简历，是选择轨迹。他放弃了什么？在上升通道什么位置离开？
②三个 Why为什么这个行业？为什么创业？为什么是现在？
③能力结构最突出的能力与证据（非自评）；最大的短板，打算怎么补
④理解深度能否画出行业地图？能否描绘技术知识树？
⑤性格主观你喜欢他什么？不喜欢他什么？两个都要答；只有喜欢没有不喜欢，说明还没真正看这个人
⑥Founder-Market Fit想做 × 能做 × 可做 × 该做——三者交集才是真正值得押注的

输出：yes / no / not yet，不允许模糊。

看事（六步）

#步骤核心追问
①亲身体验必须亲自使用/体验产品。体验改变了你的判断吗？
②行业位置放进行业地图：上游下游、传统 vs 科技 vs 新锐
③三个凭什么用户核心价值 / 团队核心能力 / 竞争核心驱动力
④能力三分科研技术 / 工程执行 / 产品商业
⑤红黄绿灯🟢 投资冲动因何触发？🔴 最可能死在哪？🟡 下一步 3 个检查点
⑥化繁为简一句话 + 一页纸。写不出来就是还没理解

四个思维动作

  • 卸妆 —— 去掉所有未来愿景，今天实际交付什么？真收入多少？
  • 画地图 —— 吃哪一段价值？最怕谁从哪个方向切进来？
  • 上时间轴 —— 放在 3 年后的行业里，还有没有位置？
  • 逼输出 —— 一句话 + 一页纸，否则不算理解

事实验证标签

✅ Verified⚠️ Unverified❌ Contradicted🔲 Gap
公开来源确认创始人说法未证与公开来源矛盾重要信息缺失

反模式（五条要避开）

  1. 后视镜思维 —— 用过去 pattern 评估未来。Pattern 会断
  2. 机车思维 —— 只盯一个 feature，不看整个系统
  3. 痛点谬误 —— AI 时代，itch point 和 cool point 同样重要
  4. Checklist 崇拜 —— Checklist 筛选不决策；决策围绕 thesis
  5. 复述市场共识 —— 观点完全来自新闻头条，回报也只能平均值

Part 2 · Thinking

Foundational Proposition · The Boundary of Human-Machine Collaboration

Everything computable should be handed to AI; humans should do the uncomputable — most centrally, choice.

AI HandlesHumans Handle
Standard answers · Fact-gathering · Data denoisingDefining the problem · Investment aesthetics · Final choice
Logic consistency checks · Historical backtestsIdentifying life force and potential that data cannot quantify
Choice is the human's first right; responsibility is the second. AI does not replace human decisions — AI makes human judgment more penetrating.

Core Belief · Investment = The Monetization of Cognition

  • Without above-average cognition → no excess returns
  • Mediocre consensus → mediocre returns
  • Monetizing cognition = "being right on the non-consensus"
"Without uniqueness, cognition has no value; without subjectivity, judgment has no force."

Three Principles · Defending "Grounded Subjectivity"

  1. Reject mediocre objectivity — Don't chase the herd's statistical correctness; mine the details only you've observed
  2. Build individual judgment labs — The organization doesn't unify cognition; it equips each person with resources and AI tools. Cognitive resonance, not cognitive uniformity
  3. Organizational capability = the sum and emergence of individual cognition — Individual uniqueness is the spark; organizational systematicity is the flame

Investor's Three Rights

RightEssence
Right to be wrongInvestment ability is built on the pain of real mistakes. Making mistakes is the norm, not the exception
Right to be ignorantCourage to admit ignorance in most fields; focus limited attention on a few. Without unique cognition, no investment career is possible
Right to delay and oscillateAllow oscillation between "invest / pass." This is not weakness — it is the brain tuning itself on high-dimensional information

Mental Disciplines (Five)

#DisciplineEssence
1People FirstAt pre-seed, only "people" can be truly evaluated; product is a hypothesis, market is a bet
2Factor HumilityEvery framework is a hypothesis. An analysis that gives clean answers too quickly is a warning, not a green light
3Causal DecompositionDon't ask "will this succeed?" Ask "what must be true?" — recursively, until falsifiable
4Coordination LayerWho has already identified this person as a winner? Can Llama amplify the structure, or are we just a passenger?
5Intellectual HonestyFacts ≠ opinions — always label. Judgment without a causal chain is only feeling

Part 3 · Method

DD Dual-Track Framework · Business / People

Looking at People (Six Steps)

#StepCore Question
①Founder BackstoryNot a resume — a trajectory of choices. What did they give up? At what position on the ascending path did they leave?
②Three WhysWhy this industry? Why entrepreneurship? Why now?
③Capability StructureStrongest ability + evidence (not self-assessment); biggest gap + how they plan to compensate
④Depth of UnderstandingCan they draw the industry map? Can they sketch the technology knowledge tree?
⑤Character & Subjective ReadWhat do you like about them? What do you dislike? Both are required — if there's only "like" and no "dislike," you haven't really seen them
⑥Founder-Market FitWant × Can × Allowed × Should — only the intersection is worth betting on

Output: yes / no / not yet — no ambiguity allowed.

Looking at Business (Six Steps)

#StepCore Question
①First-Hand ExperienceMust personally use or experience the product. Did the experience change your judgment?
②Industry PositionPlace on the industry map: upstream / downstream, traditional vs tech vs new-wave
③Three "Why Them"User core value / Team core capability / Competitive core driver
④Capability TriadResearch-tech / Engineering-execution / Product-business
⑤Red-Yellow-Green🟢 What triggered the investment urge? 🔴 Where is it most likely to die? 🟡 Three checkpoints for next step
⑥DistillationOne sentence + one page. If you can't write it, you haven't understood

Four Mental Moves

  • De-makeup — Strip all future vision. What does it actually deliver today? What's real revenue?
  • Draw the map — Which slice of value does it eat? Who can attack it from which direction?
  • Push on the timeline — Place it 3 years into the industry's future. Does it still have a seat?
  • Force output — One sentence + one page, or it doesn't count as understanding

Anti-Patterns (Five to Avoid)

  1. Rearview-mirror thinking — Using past patterns to evaluate the future. Patterns break
  2. Locomotive thinking — Fixating on one feature, missing the whole system
  3. Pain-point fallacy — In the AI era, itch-points and cool-points matter equally
  4. Checklist worship — Checklists filter; they don't decide. Decisions orbit thesis
  5. Repeating market consensus — Views sourced entirely from headlines; returns capped at average
